Copy blocked, or pasted rows look plainer than the rest?
  • Copy blocked: click the box below and press Ctrl/Cmd+C.
  • Borders, fonts, colours: this tool copies values, not formatting — those come from your sheet. Apply borders to a wide range once (say A4:AY1000, via the Name box) and every future paste will sit inside them. Or copy one formatted row and use Paste special → Format only on the new rows.
